## 3. Account Creation & Security Protocol

1. **Dedicated Email:** Create or dedicate an email alias/inbox (e.g. `soumyadeepsarkar2004@outlook.com` or `contact@anusandhan.ai`).
2. **2-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Enable Authenticator App (Google Authenticator / 1Password) on all accounts.
3. **Cross-Linking:** Link all platforms back to `https://anusandhan.vercel.app` and `https://github.com/soumyadeepsarkar-2004/Anusandhan`.
4. **Creator Promotion Policy:** Once official channels are initialized, personal profiles (such as Soumyadeep's LinkedIn and `@sarkar58153` on X) can repost/quote-tweet official announcements to jumpstart initial algorithmic reach.
