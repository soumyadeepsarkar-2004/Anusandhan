# Creative Production Suite: 25-Skill Ecosystem

This suite integrates the 25 high-leverage open-source skills identified by [@Y0urDumble](https://x.com/Y0urDumble/status/2120197632500887856) (860K+ GitHub Stars combined) into our autonomous development and marketing workflows.

---

## 1. DESIGN
1. **anthropic-skills** (`github.com/anthropics/skills`): Official frontend & canvas design patterns.
2. **impeccable** (`github.com/pbakaus/impeccable`): `/polish`, `/bolder`, `/critique` to eliminate generic AI design artifacts.
3. **open-design** (`github.com/nexu-io/open-design`): Open-source AI design workspace.
4. **hallmark** (`github.com/Nutlope/hallmark`): High-taste design rules preventing "AI-made" aesthetics (*active in workspace*).
5. **huashu-design** (`github.com/alchaincyf/huashu`): One-line prompt in, finished production design out.

---

## 2. VIDEO
6. **hyperframes** (`github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes`): Write HTML/CSS, get high-definition video.
7. **openmontage** (`github.com/OpenMontageInc/openmontage`): Agentic video editing studio with 100+ tools.
8. **video-shotcraft** (`github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft`): Cinematic product promo direction.
9. **remotion-skills** (`github.com/remotion-dev/skills`): Official Remotion video generation skills for React.
10. **animated-shorts** (`github.com/mromidev/claude-animated-shorts`): Viral 3D-world cartoon automation.

---

## 3. 3D
11. **blender-mcp** (`github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender`): Full agent integration with Blender via MCP (`look` viewport inspections & Poly Haven CC0 asset search).
12. **anatomy** (`github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy`): Convert concepts into working 3D objects.
13. **unity-mcp** (`github.com/CoplayDev/unity-mcp`): Drives and automates the Unity Editor.
14. **universal-modder** (`github.com/rehan-remade/universal-modder`): Mod PC game environments and assets.
15. **gamedev-skills** (`github.com/gamedev-skills`): 77 game development skills from Godot to Three.js.

---

## 4. MOTION
16. **gsap-skills** (`github.com/greensock/gsap`): Official easing, stagger, and scroll-driven motion.
17. **garden-skills** (`github.com/ConardLi/garden-skills`): Motion showreels and interactive web video decks.
18. **pixelfx** (`github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx`): 244 procedural pixel and shader effects in code.
19. **anything2explainer** (`github.com/Vincentwei1021/anything2explainer`): Topic in, motion explainer video out.
20. **oil-motion** (`github.com/oil-oil/oil-motion`): Organic scroll- and mouse-reactive motion.

---

## 5. BRAND
21. **guizang-ppt** (`github.com/op7418/guizang`): Editorial magazine and Swiss-style presentation decks.
22. **stitch-skills** (`github.com/google-labs-co/stitch-skills`): Google UI design system skills (*integrated via MCP*).
23. **architecture-diagrams** (`github.com/Cocoon-AI/architecture-diagrams`): Technical system architecture visualization.
24. **logo-design** (`github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design`): Brief -> SVG logo -> complete brand guidelines.
25. **nothing-design** (`github.com/dominikmartn/nothing-design`): Monochrome dot-matrix typography & UI.

---

## Usage Guidelines
- Proactively call these skills when generating client deliverables, launch trailers, social graphics, 3D product renders, or architectural diagrams.
- Always apply `.agents/rules/motion_engineering.md` whenever generating or reviewing animations.
