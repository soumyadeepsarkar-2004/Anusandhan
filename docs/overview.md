# Platform Overview

## Vision & Problem Statement

Every summer, tens of thousands of ambitious university students cold-email university professors seeking research internships and funded positions (e.g., DAAD WISE, Mitacs Globalink, CERN Summer Student, TIFR VSRP). 

Historically, this has resulted in:
1. **Generic AI Spam**: Students mass-blast 100+ generic emails generated by rudimentary ChatGPT templates ("Dear Respected Sir, I love your esteemed research...").
2. **Domain Blacklisting**: University spam filters flag `.edu` and personal Gmail addresses as spam due to burst cold outreach without pacing.
3. **Ghost Rates**: Over 97% of faculty delete cold emails within 3 seconds because the student offers zero proof-of-work or paper comprehension.

**Anusandhan (अनुसंधान)** transforms this dynamic by engineering a scientific, calibrated academic matchmaking platform backed by the **AnusandhanAI** autonomous assistant.

## Core Pillars of the Platform

### 1. Research Readiness Score (RRS™)
An objective, 0–100 benchmark auditing candidate credentials across 5 weighted dimensions:

| Pillar | Max Points | Measurement Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Code Execution & Benchmarks** | 25 | Public GitHub repositories with reproducible environment configurations (Docker, Conda, requirements), unit tests, and performance benchmarks. |
| **Research Methodology** | 25 | Understanding of baseline ablation, empirical hypothesis framing, and academic publication standards (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, Nature). |
| **Academic Foundations** | 20 | Core coursework mastery (Linear Algebra, Algorithms, Systems), verified CGPA/GPA, and institutional affiliation. |
| **Lab Tooling & Frameworks** | 15 | Hands-on experience with hardware/software accelerators (PyTorch, CUDA, JAX, HuggingFace, Slurm, TensorRT). |
| **Professionalism & Ethics** | 15 | Clear communication, concise inquiries, zero exaggeration, and respect for faculty bandwidth. |

### 2. 146-Word Precision Proof-of-Work Standard
Generic emails are strictly rejected. Inquiries synthesized by Anusandhan adhere to a strict 146-word length requirement:
- **Hook (Sentence 1)**: Cites an exact verified paper published by the professor between 2024 and 2026.
- **Limitation (Sentence 2)**: Pinpoints the specific algorithmic bottleneck, dataset constraint, or hardware limitation noted in the paper's conclusion.
- **Bridge (Sentence 3-4)**: Connects the limitation to the student's verified GitHub codebase, offering a runnable reproduction script or proposed ablation experiment.
- **Ask (Sentence 5)**: Requests a 12-minute technical Zoom screen or async code review.

### 3. Ethical Academic Outreach Guardrails
To protect student and university email reputations:
- **Daily Quota**: Strictly limited to a maximum of 5 cold outreach emails per 24 hours.
- **Jitter Delays**: Enforces randomized 4–12 minute delays between dispatches to mimic natural human typing.
- **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop**: Zero emails are sent automatically without manual student preview and single-click approval.
- **7-Day Polite Cadence**: Follow-ups are limited to a single nudge after a mandatory 7-calendar-day waiting period.
